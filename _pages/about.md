I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at [CREST (Center for Research in Economics and Statistics)](https://www.ensae.fr/en/research/center-research-economics-and-statistics-crest), working on the Horizon Europe project [Incognito Justice](https://www.incojust.eu). I am also a Research Fellow at [Tor Vergata CEIS (Centre for Economic and International Studies)](https://ceistorvergata.it) and an Associated Researcher at [Luiss CESARE Laboratory for Experimental Economics](https://www.luiss.edu/research/research-centers/laboratory-experimental-economics-cesare). I completed my PhD in Economics at the University of Innsbruck.

Using field experiments, I investigate how economic incentives and behavioral biases influence decisions in markets that impact sustainability and social welfare. My broader research agenda includes prosocial behavior and gender economics.
